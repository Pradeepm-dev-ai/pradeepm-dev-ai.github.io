Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

yt-autoposter is a personal automation tool used only by its developer. It is not distributed to or used by any other people.

Data accessed

With the developer's own permission, the tool reads video files from the developer's Google Drive (read-only access) and uploads them to the developer's own YouTube channel.

Data collection and sharing

The tool does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of any other person. Login credentials are kept only in the developer's private settings and are never disclosed to third parties.

Google API Services

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
